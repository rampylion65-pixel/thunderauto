Privacy policy

This private publishing workspace stores profile names, uploaded videos, scheduling metadata, publishing results and encrypted social authorization tokens to publish content on authorized accounts. It uses Render, Supabase, Cloudflare R2 and the connected social platform APIs. Records and files remain until removed by the administrator. Published content is subject to the destination platform's policies.

{% elif kind=='terms' %}

Terms of service

Use this workspace only for accounts and content you are authorized to manage. You are responsible for content rights, audience settings and platform compliance. Publishing may be delayed or fail. This is a private testing service, not a paid public service.

{% else %}

Data deletion instructions

In Social accounts, disconnect Facebook to remove its stored connection. Revoke Thunder access in your Facebook or Google account settings as appropriate. In the queue, delete unwanted jobs; in Settings, clean published files or clear published history. For complete deletion of remaining account records and tokens, contact the workspace administrator. Removing workspace records does not remove posts already published on social platforms.
